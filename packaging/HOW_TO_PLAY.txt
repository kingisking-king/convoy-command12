CONVOY COMMAND
Double-click ConvoyCommand.exe. No install.

Win a mission by getting at least one cargo truck to the drop.
Lose if every cargo truck is destroyed.
Pay from a win is added to your war chest for the next mission.

BUILD
  Place vehicles anywhere in the yard at the start of the road.
  There is no lane cap. The column keeps that spacing as it drives.
  1-9, 0, - and = select the unit cards
  Left click empty ground to place the selected unit
  Left click a vehicle, then drag it
  Missions unlock in order. Sandbox can use any map.
  Right click remove
  R          rotate the selected vehicle
  Q          suggested wedge
  Enter      deploy
  Side panel: convoy name, camo, armor / weapon / speed upgrades,
              Line / Wedge / Box, save and load presets
  Right-drag orbit, wheel zoom, F reset camera
  A/D orbit, W/S zoom

Bring at least one cargo truck. Put guns beside or ahead of the cargo.
Anti-air is for helicopters. Do not lead with cargo.

DRIVE
  Guns fire automatically. The column keeps moving if you take a gun.
  G          man the nearest gun, or leave it
  Click      an armed vehicle to man that gun
  Mouse      aim
  Left click fire
  Right click scope
  Wheel      switch weapon (tank main gun and coax)
  Tab        next gun
  Q smoke     thick cover, enemies miss more
  E airstrike a jet, then a blast. Hostiles only
  R field repair
  Esc pause
  Right-drag orbit, wheel zoom, F recenter
  F11 fullscreen, M mute

Yellow on the map is cargo. Red is hostile.

SANDBOX
  From the main menu. Unlimited money, every unit, and every upgrade.
  Pick Dust Road, Pine Cut, or High Pass.
  Set which enemies attack, and how many.
  Difficulty slider, or Easy / Normal / Hard / Brutal.
  God mode keeps the convoy from taking damage.
  Speed slider changes how fast the drive runs.
  Left click the ground to spawn the selected enemy.
  Restart Drive runs the same column again immediately.
  Sandbox does not change campaign progress.
